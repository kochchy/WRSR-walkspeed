[h1]Walkspeed Plugin[/h1]
A plugin for [b]TesmioLoader[/b] that allows you to configure the walking speed and travel time limits of your citizens.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Walk Speed Scaling[/b]: Globally scale citizen walking speeds (make them walk 50% faster or slower) while preserving the natural vanilla variance (0.9x to 1.1x).
[*] [b]Travel Timer Dampening[/b]: Optionally dampen the internal state-timers to extend the maximum travel/waiting times, allowing citizens to undertake longer journeys without timing out and teleporting home.
[/list]

[h2]Installation[/h2]
[b]Important:[/b] This mod requires [url=https://github.com/Tesmio/TesmioLoader]TesmioLoader[/url] to work! Subscribing to this workshop item will download the files to your workshop folder, but you must manually install the plugin.
[list=1]
[*] Ensure you have the TesmioLoader framework installed.
[*] Locate the downloaded files in your Steam workshop directory (usually [code]Steam\steamapps\workshop\content\784150\...\)[/code]
[*] Copy [code]walkspeed.dll[/code] and [code]walkspeed.ini[/code] to your game directory: [code]Steam\steamapps\common\SovietRepublic\tesmioloader\build\plugins\[/code]
[*] Activate the plugin via the [code]tesmiolauncher.exe[/code] interface.
[/list]

[h2]Configuration[/h2]
Edit [code]walkspeed.ini[/code] in your plugins folder to adjust the multipliers. The file is fully commented in English.
